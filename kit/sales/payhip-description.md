## Description

**What it is**

The Faceless Creator Kit is a web app that runs in your browser. It has two tools:

**1. Channel Planner**
- A board for every video: Idea → Script → Voice/Avatar → Edit → Thumbnail → Scheduled → Published. As many channels as you like, each with its own color, niche, product links and keywords.
- **Prompt builder** with three ready-to-use templates (listicle explainer, story/insider reveal, how-to). Fill in your points, copy the prompt, paste it into the AI tool you use for scripts. Edit the templates or add your own, with a fixed ending line.
- **Description builder:** hook, your product links, chapters (checked against YouTube's chapter rules), disclaimer and hashtags — copied in one click.
- **Title & thumbnail lab:** compare three titles and thumbnail texts. Each title gets a simple score out of 100 and every point is explained, so you know exactly why.
- **Shorts planner:** turn your chapters into a list of Shorts with hooks, on-screen text and titles.
- Calendar, a topics bank with a "this sounds like a video you already made" warning, and a weekly review where you type in your numbers and see small trend lines.

**2. Pin Factory**
- **8 pin templates** at Pinterest's 2:3 size (1000 × 1500): Bold Headline, Numbered List, Quick Tip, Recipe/Product Card, Quote, Before → After / Do vs Don't, Money Compare and Checklist.
- **Brand kit** per channel: your colors, 6 included fonts, your logo or photo in a circle, your website or handle and a call-to-action tag.
- **Batch mode:** paste rows from Google Sheets or Excel (or upload a CSV) and render all pins at once. Click any pin to fix it.
- **Export:** all images as PNG or JPG (under 350 KB each) in one ZIP, plus a **Pinterest bulk-upload CSV** with your public image links, UTM-tagged links, a posting schedule (pins per day, times, similar pins spread apart) and title/description length checks. The included guide shows how to host your images for free with GitHub Pages.

## FAQ

**Is this a subscription?**
No. One payment, and you keep access to the app.

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. It opens in your browser. You can optionally install it like an app from the browser so it has its own window and icon.

**Where is my data stored? Can you see it?**
Only in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. That also means you should download a backup now and then (one click).

**Can I use it on my phone and my computer?**
Yes. Each device keeps its own data; move it with Backup → Restore.

**Does it work for more than one channel?**
Yes, add as many channels as you like, each with its own products, keywords and pin branding.

**Which AI tool do the prompts work with?**
Any chat-style AI tool you already use for scripts. The kit builds the prompt; you paste it where you like.

**Does it post to Pinterest for me?**
No. It makes the pin images and the CSV file that Pinterest's own bulk-upload accepts. You upload the CSV in your Pinterest account.

**Can I use the pins commercially?**
Yes. The pins you make are yours. The included fonts are open-source (SIL Open Font License).
