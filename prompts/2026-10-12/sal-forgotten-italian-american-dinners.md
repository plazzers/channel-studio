AUDIENCE: US home cooks 40-75, budget-minded families and seniors, people who love old-school Italian-American food.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Plain spoken script only, ready for voice generation. No stage directions.
STRUCTURE: Hook under 1 minute that ends with a promise, numbered segments, mid-video free PDF mention, payoff, outro with product mention and tease of the next video.
1. Hook: before takeout menus, many Italian-American families fed a big table on beans, bread, eggs and a little meat on Sunday. These dinners are cheap, cozy and almost forgotten. Promise: 25 of them, what each one is, and one tip to make it great — grouped so viewers can pick tonight's dinner.
2. Part 1 — Beans and greens (6): pasta e fagioli; pasta e ceci (chickpeas); escarole and beans; beans and greens with garlic; lentil soup with small pasta; minestrone made from whatever vegetables are left.
3. Part 2 — Eggs for dinner (5): peppers and eggs; potatoes and eggs; eggs in purgatory (eggs poached in tomato sauce); leftover-spaghetti frittata; pastina with egg and cheese.
4. Mid-video: the free 25 Rules for Eating Out is the first link in the description.
5. Part 3 — Pasta from the pantry (6): spaghetti aglio e olio; pasta with toasted breadcrumbs ("poor man's Parmesan"); pasta with peas; pasta with cauliflower; pasta with lentils; pasta with butter, cheese and black pepper.
6. Part 4 — Sunday and stretch-the-meat dinners (5): braciole in Sunday sauce; sausage and peppers; chicken cacciatore; stuffed peppers with rice and a little meat; polenta with sauce and sausage.
7. Part 5 — Holiday and old-world (3): escarole soup with little meatballs (often called wedding soup); stuffed artichokes; baccalà (salt cod) salad from the holiday table.
8. For each dish: one or two sentences on what it is, why it was cheap, and Sal's one tip (save the Parmesan rind for the soup, toast the breadcrumbs in olive oil, cook garlic gently, save the pasta water). No full recipes or amounts.
9. Payoff: these dinners stretch a little money a long way. Pick three for next week's menu.
10. Outro: Sal's Italian Kitchen for full Italian recipes; quick mention of the Complete Kitchen bundle in the description; tease next video.
