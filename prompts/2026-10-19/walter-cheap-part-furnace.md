1. Hook: the furnace is the most expensive thing in the basement, and one of the cheapest parts decides how hard it works all winter: the air filter. Most people only think about it when something goes wrong. Promise: what the filter does, how to check it in a minute, and the 6 mistakes people make with it.
2. What the filter does: it protects the blower and the inside of the furnace from dust, and it helps keep the air in the house cleaner. When it clogs, the furnace has to pull air through it, like breathing through a towel. Many furnaces then run longer and can overheat and shut themselves off as a safety step.
3. How to find it: usually in a slot near the blower compartment, in the return air duct next to the furnace, or behind a return grille in a wall or ceiling. Turn the thermostat or the furnace off before you slide it out.
4. The 1-minute check: hold it up to a light. If you can't see light through it, or it's grey and fuzzy, it's time. Write the date on the edge of the new one.
5. Mistake 1: putting it in backwards. The arrow on the frame points toward the furnace, in the direction of the air flow.
6. Mistake 2: the wrong size. Read the size printed on the old filter's frame (and the furnace manual). A filter that's loose lets dusty air go around it.
7. Mid-video: the free Weekend Home Check is the first link in the description; the filter is one of its checks.
8. Mistake 3: going by the calendar only. The box may say up to a certain number of months, but pets, construction dust, a new house or a lot of run time can fill it much faster. Check monthly in the heating season.
9. Mistake 4: assuming the thickest, highest-rated filter is always best. Very restrictive filters can be too much for some systems. Check the furnace manual or ask the HVAC company what rating their system is designed for. Say this carefully: "check what your system is made for".
10. Mistake 5: forgetting there may be more than one. Some houses have several return grilles with filters, or a filter at the furnace and one in a wall.
11. Mistake 6: running the furnace with no filter "just for a day". Dust goes straight into the blower and the coil.
12. When it's not the filter: a clean filter and still weak heat, odd smells, short cycling, or the furnace keeps shutting off — call a licensed HVAC technician. A yearly tune-up before winter is something many makers recommend. Gas smell: leave the house and call the gas company from outside.
13. Payoff: the three-second habit — every time you pay a utility bill in winter, look at the filter. Light through it, arrow toward the furnace, date on the frame.
14. Outro: Walter's Home Check App reminds you when the filter is due; tease the next video (7 things careful homeowners do every fall).
