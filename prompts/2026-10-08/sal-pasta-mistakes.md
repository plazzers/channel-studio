1. Hook: same box of pasta, same jar of tomatoes, and the restaurant plate still tastes better. It's not magic, it's seven small habits. Promise: fix them tonight.
2. Mistake 1: Too small a pot, too little water. Pasta needs room to move; stir in the first minute or two.
3. Mistake 2: Oil in the water. It mostly floats and makes the sauce slide off later.
4. Mistake 3: Under-seasoned water. Salt the water well — taste it — but don't give exact grams; say "it should taste seasoned".
5. Mistake 4: Pouring all the pasta water down the drain. Save a mug; the starch makes sauce silky and helps it cling.
6. Mid-video: the free 25 Rules for Eating Out is the first link in the description.
7. Mistake 5: Sauce on top of naked pasta. Pull the pasta a minute early and finish it in the pan with the sauce and a splash of pasta water.
8. Mistake 6: Burnt garlic and cheese added on high heat. Garlic over gentle heat until pale gold; cheese off the heat, a little at a time, freshly grated.
9. Mistake 7: Rinsing pasta for a hot dish, and serving on cold plates. Don't rinse; warm the plates.
10. Payoff: the 30-second checklist — big pot, no oil, taste the water, save a mug, finish in the pan, gentle garlic, cheese off the heat.
11. Outro: Sal's Italian Kitchen (60 real Italian recipes) for the full sauces and pastas; mention the Copycat Cookbook's Sunday Marinara; tease next video.
