TITLE: Restaurant Tricks for a Stress-Free Thanksgiving Dinner
NARRATOR: Chef Sal Romano, a high-energy, confident Italian-American TV chef speaking to camera in first person.
AUDIENCE: US home cooks and people who eat out, 30-65, hosting a holiday dinner for family.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Plain spoken script only, ready for voice generation. No stage directions.
STRUCTURE: Hook under 1 minute that ends with a promise, numbered segments, mid-video free PDF mention, payoff, outro with product mention and tease of the next video.
1. Hook: a restaurant kitchen serves hundreds of plates a night without panicking; your kitchen panics for twelve. The difference isn't talent, it's a system. Promise: 7 restaurant tricks that make the big dinner calmer and tastier.
2. Trick 1: Prep list and timeline. Restaurants work backward from service time. Write the dinner time at the bottom and plan back.
3. Trick 2: Mise en place. Chop, measure and line everything up before the heat goes on.
4. Trick 3: Make sauces ahead. Gravy the day before with butter, flour and good stock; reheat gently and whisk in pan drippings on the day. Cranberry sauce days ahead.
5. Trick 4: Season at every step, and taste as you go.
6. Mid-video: the free 25 Rules for Eating Out is the first link in the description (good for the holiday dinners out, too).
7. Trick 5: Let the turkey (and any roast) rest before carving so more juice stays in the meat; that resting time is when the rolls and sides go in the oven. Follow a reliable food-safety source for doneness temperatures; don't state temperatures from memory.
8. Trick 6: The bread basket. Soft honey butter dinner rolls like the steakhouse basket, baked a day ahead and rewarmed in foil, with whipped honey butter (soft butter, honey, pinch of cinnamon, pinch of salt). No full recipe amounts.
9. Trick 7: Warm the plates and set up a "pass" — a clear counter where plates get finished, like a restaurant.
10. Payoff: the night-before list — gravy done, rolls baked, table set, prep list on the fridge.
11. Outro: Sal's Restaurant Copycat Cookbook (steakhouse rolls, sides, sauces) and the Complete Kitchen bundle; tease next video on pasta mistakes.
