1. Hook: in the old Italian kitchen, the bread that went stale became dinner, the cheese rind went in the soup, and the Sunday sauce fed the week. Nothing was wasted because nothing could be. Promise: 10 of those habits that still work in a modern fridge, and they save real money on groceries.
2. Trick 1: the Parmesan rind. Keep rinds in a bag in the freezer and drop one in soup, beans or sauce while it simmers; take it out before serving.
3. Trick 2: stale bread is an ingredient. Toasted breadcrumbs for pasta, panzanella in summer, ribollita and bread soups in winter. Freeze bread you won't finish rather than leaving it to mold.
4. Trick 3: cook the sauce once, eat it all week. A big pot of marinara frozen in portions, labeled with the date. Mention Sal's free Sunday Marinara recipe on the guide site.
5. Trick 4: herbs in oil, frozen. Chop leftover parsley or basil, cover with olive oil in an ice cube tray and freeze. Pop one into a pan. (Store any herbs-in-oil frozen or refrigerated and use within a few days; never on the counter.)
6. Trick 5: the scrap bag. Onion ends, carrot peels, celery tops and parsley stems in a freezer bag; when it's full, it becomes broth.
7. Mid-video: the free 25 Rules for Eating Out is the first link in the description.
8. Trick 6: buy the whole chicken. Roast it one night, use leftovers in pasta or soup the next, and simmer the bones into broth.
9. Trick 7: dried beans in big batches. Cook a pot, freeze in portions with some cooking liquid. Pasta e fagioli and beans with greens are always one step away.
10. Trick 8: zest before you squeeze. Zest lemons and oranges before juicing and freeze the zest. Juice can be frozen in cubes too.
11. Trick 9: leftover pasta becomes a frittata. The Naples frittata di pasta: cold pasta, eggs, grated cheese, a hot pan, flip.
12. Trick 10: the "eat me first" shelf. One spot in the fridge for things that need to be used soon; plan the next dinner around it. First in, first out in the pantry.
13. Food safety, short and clear: refrigerate leftovers promptly, reheat until steaming hot, label freezer bags with dates, and when in doubt, throw it out.
14. Payoff: the 10 in one line each — rind, stale bread, sauce once, herbs in oil, scrap bag, whole chicken, beans, zest, pasta frittata, eat-me-first shelf. Waste nothing, eat well.
