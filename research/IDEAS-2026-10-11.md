# YouTube Idea Research: Walter's Home Check + Chef Sal Romano

Generated 2026-10-11 from NexLev data (faceless outlier feed, keyword video search, YouTube search, channel outliers). Raw evidence is in `evidence.json`.
Outlier score = video views / that channel's average views (3+ is exceptional). Almost all examples are from channels under 100k subs, published Feb–Oct 2026, English.

**Data caveat:** NexLev has very little Shorts outlier data for these exact niches. Keyword `search_videos` for Shorts mostly returned brand ads. So the Shorts ideas lean on the long-form winners plus a few Shorts formats from nearby niches that worked (e.g. "Why THIS house…", "The secret of the…", "$10 vs $1,000").
